Renewable energy systems use naturally replenished resources such as sunlight, wind, and flowing water. Solar panels convert sunlight into electrical energy, while wind turbines use moving air to generate power.

Energy storage can help address the variable output of renewable sources. Batteries and other storage technologies allow electricity to be retained and supplied when generation changes.

The transition toward renewable energy involves technical, economic, and environmental considerations. Efficient systems require appropriate planning, reliable infrastructure, and careful management of energy resources.
