Artificial intelligence is transforming modern healthcare through machine learning systems that can assist doctors in analysing medical data. These systems can identify patterns in patient records and support clinical decision-making.

Artificial intelligence is also being applied to education, finance, manufacturing, and software development. Modern systems can process large datasets, recognize patterns, and automate selected tasks.

Reliable data and careful evaluation are essential when artificial intelligence is used in practical applications. Automated systems should be evaluated before their outputs are used to support important decisions.
